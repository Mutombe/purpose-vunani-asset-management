{{icon:vault}}Solution 02

Cash Management

Optimise liquidity and operational efficiency. Our treasury team manages the placing of fixed-term deposits on behalf of clients, and this is how we protect every one of them.

  1. {{icon:magnifying-glass}}01

    Every bank researched

    Extensive market research on all banks we invest with, plus background checks on senior personnel. We only deal with selected banks that meet our criteria.

  2. {{icon:scales}}02

    Rates compared

    Our treasury team reviews all maturing deposits and obtains indicative interest rates from a number of selected banks before recommending a renewal.

  3. {{icon:hand-coins}}03

    Pooled for better pricing

    Pooling deposits and dealing directly with banks' treasury departments lets us pass on competitive money market pricing.

  4. {{icon:shield-check}}04

    Secured and monitored

    Appropriate security is obtained from each bank as cover for deposits. Counterparty credit risk is monitored continuously, with regular cash portfolio reports.
