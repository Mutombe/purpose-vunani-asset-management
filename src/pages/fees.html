{{icon:scales}}How is my band decided?

Your equity fee band is set by the size of your investment. As your portfolio grows into a higher band, the lower annual rate applies.

{{icon:coins}}What currency are fees charged in?

Fees are charged in the currency of your account. The calculator above is indicative and shows the same figures in any currency.

{{icon:chart-line-up}}How does the performance fee work?

On fixed interest mandates we charge 10% of the value we create for you. The remaining 90% of that value is yours.

{{icon:file-pdf}}Where is my fee schedule written down?

Your exact fee schedule is confirmed in your signed mandate. You can download the mandate forms from our documents page.
