The investment cycle

Select a stage on the dial or in the list to see what happens at each step. The cycle advances on its own until you take the wheel.

01Screening

Screening

Extensive quantitative and qualitative screening of securities. The team meets daily to discuss opportunities and allocate tasks.

  • Quantitative screens
  • Qualitative review
  • Daily opportunity meetings

Research

Rigorous due diligence on management strength, balance sheet, earnings quality, industry structure and ESG, supported by detailed bottom-up valuations.

  • Management & balance sheet
  • Earnings quality
  • ESG due diligence

Portfolio construction

Recommended securities are weighed against our top-down view, portfolio characteristics and sector correlation, with monthly reviews for oversight.

  • Top-down view
  • Sector correlation
  • Monthly reviews

Risk management

Continuous assessment of macro-economic conditions and performance, with scenario analysis that tracks changes in each client's liquidity needs.

  • Macro monitoring
  • Scenario analysis
  • Liquidity tracking

Implementation

Asset allocation and security selection build and rebalance your portfolio, adding new investment vehicles as they become available.

  • Asset allocation
  • Security selection
  • Ongoing rebalancing

Four lenses on
every security

Our approach is grounded in fundamental research and complemented by macro-economic, technical and quantitative analysis.

01

Fundamental

Management strength, balance sheet, earnings quality and industry structure, with detailed bottom-up valuations.

02

Macro-economic

A top-down view of the Zimbabwean and regional economy that shapes asset allocation and sector weights.

03

Technical

Price, volume and liquidity signals that inform the timing of entries, exits and rebalancing.

04

Quantitative

Systematic screens and scenario analysis that test every idea against the numbers.

“We offer discretionary (DS) and non-discretionary (NDS) stock portfolio management.”

You choose how involved you want to be

Two ways to
work with us

Both mandates run through the same five-stage process. The difference is who signs off each decision.

DS

Discretionary

You agree the mandate, objectives and limits with us. Our team then makes and executes investment decisions within them, so your portfolio can move when markets do.

NDS

Non-discretionary

We research and recommend, and you approve each decision before we act. Ideal for investors who want to stay hands-on.

Risk

Monitored continuously

Macro-economic conditions, performance and counterparty credit risk are assessed on an ongoing basis.

Oversight

Reviewed monthly

Portfolios are reviewed every month against their mandate, characteristics and sector correlation.

Reporting

Reported regularly

Regular portfolio reports keep you informed on holdings, cash and performance.
