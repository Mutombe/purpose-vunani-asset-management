Purpose Vunani Asset Management is registered by the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe as Purpose Asset Management (Private) Limited, and is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe.

Our mandate is to meet our clients' current income and investment growth needs. Whatever the market conditions, we seek to deliver superior and consistent performance, develop products for diverse needs, provide the highest quality service and ensure regulatory compliance.
